## What the resources actually mean

- Iron `1008` buys for 3 gold and sells for 1. These 5 iron can avoid **up to 15 gold of future purchases**, if needed, or realize 5 gold **only if sold**. Neither occurred on return; cash income was zero. Leather similarly buys for 3/sells for 1; steel buys for 15/sells for 5 where purchasing is actually unlocked.
- Food `1005` has no market price or resale worth and is production-only. Two farmers provide the two wheat consumed by one cook for one food per 20-second cycle. Eight food correspond to 160 seconds of this production capacity (120 for movement, 40 for discarded cargo), not a retail bill or necessarily an extra wait. Production can overlap other play and the profile already had ample reserves.
- Owned iron mine `3068` offers fixed 5 iron via the materials callback, subject to collection/cargo. This is a resource loop. Initial conquest uses two skeletons and unlocks forge `57` and iron mine `61`; repeat collection is not a new unlock.
- Building the unlocked iron mine costs 20 gold + 20 wood. One assigned iron worker consumes 2 food for 1 iron each cycle. One cook cannot sustainably feed that worker at full rate by itself, although reserves can buffer it. An unlocked building is not yet a built/staffed producer.
- New low-tier exploration can provide discoveries, occupation unlocks and specific materials, beyond coins. Skeleton `10001` and octopus `10002` have a source coin range 1–5; the reef's second enemy `10098` has 5–10 coins plus 1–2 wood. These are data ranges, not measured averages or guaranteed banked income. `getRandomNumByRange` reseeds using time; no independent/uniform-roll assumption or statistical expected-return claim is made here.

## The current choices

1. **Promote one starter crew to sailor `101`.** Recipe chain: chain armor `1040` = 22 leather; iron sword `1049` = 12 iron + 10 wood. The built forge opens both recipes (`produce` 44/43). With 5 iron, 54 wood and no leather/equipment, the missing materials cost 66 + 21 = **87 gold** at current visible market prices. With 11 current gold, that means 38 further successful yield-2 alchemy clicks if buying all missing materials. Keeping the collected iron for the sword avoids 15 gold of this purchase; selling it for 5 and rebuying later is worse. Alternatively, further mine collection or production can replace purchases.
2. **Cargo 30→40 (`1149`).** Previous cargo recipe unlocks this successor. With existing wood, 30 leather costs **90 gold**, or 40 further successful clicks from 11 gold (45 from zero). It adds 10 shared food/loot slots, useful when capacity/range is the actual constraint. It does not improve combat. The safe mine visit demonstrated a food-versus-loot choice, not proof that this upgrade is mandatory.
3. **Alchemy 2→3 (`1177`).** Previous furnace unlocks this successor, but the required 15 steel is not in the observed market. **225 gold is only hypothetical all-buy replacement cost**, not a currently available purchase plan. If later available at that price, the extra +1 gold/click repays that cost over 225 subsequent successful clicks, before considering other uses of steel. Source steel production needs its own unlock/build/input chain; the steel site `3075` has 12/13-HP, attack-3 enemies, so is not established safe for the 5-HP starter. Do not recommend grinding 225 gold as the immediate next goal.

The sailor source stats are HP 6, attack 3, speed 3.8 and attackSpeed 2.64; starter stats are HP 5, attack 1, speed 4.3 and attackSpeed 2.04. This is a different combat profile, not a guaranteed win. `TrainMode.lua` consumes one armor and one sword, replaces one starter with one sailor, and has no additional gold, villager-population or ship-slot charge in this path. `mark` affects the indicator, not a hard map gate here.
